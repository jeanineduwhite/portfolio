Vrbo push notifications

Vrbo · Content design · ~2 weeks

What I owned: Rewrote 14 push notifications to match brand guidelines, drop outdated copy, and give travelers a reason to open the app. Presented with a content design director and a PM to the President of Brands.

I joined late. The constraint was time, not a blank page: legacy strings, mixed voice, and messages written like internal status codes.

The problem in the copy

Declined because the host never responded

BeforeYour booking request has been declined because the owner (manager) failed to respond within 24 hours
AfterThe host declined your booking request. We’re sorry things didn’t work out. We can help you find more places to stay in the Vrbo app.

Host canceled

BeforeYour booking was cancelled by the owner (manager)
AfterThe host canceled your booking. We’re sorry things didn’t work out. We can help you find more places to stay in the Vrbo app.

Check-in

BeforeIt’s almost check-in time! Your check-in guide is ready in the Vrbo app.
AfterIt’s check-in time! Need help getting your stay started? Open now.

Decisions

Drop internal nouns. Travelers don’t say “owner (manager).” They have a host. “Failed to respond within 24 hours” is an ops fact. It does not help the person who just lost a stay.

Bad news needs a next step. Empathy without a path is just a softer dead end.

Don’t over-personalize confirmation on a lock screen. “All set! Your booking is confirmed.” does the job without looking like a marketing blast.

One voice across the set. Confirmation, payment, messages, check-in, decline, cancel — rewritten as a system, not 14 one-offs.
