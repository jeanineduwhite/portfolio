Travel Shops collections

Expedia · Content Designer II · 2022–2025

What I owned: UI content for creating, saving, managing, and sharing collections: labels, helper text, errors, empty states, and confirmations across desktop and iOS.

Travel Shops are shoppable storefronts. Creators (and later, anyone) save stays into collections travelers can book. The content problem was not “make it sound fun.” It was three overlapping save models (Shop, collection, trip), two audiences, and a long tail of failure states.

The job of the language

A collection is a shoppable list, not a wishlist and not a trip. If the UI said “save” everywhere, people would not know where the stay went or who could see it.

When a traveler tapped save from a property page, the sheet had to name the destination:

  • Collections: Add to a shoppable collection that you can share
  • Current picks: Add to your favorite picks on your Travel Shop
  • Trips: Add to a personal trip or collaborate with friends

Create-collection helper: Add stays to a shoppable list.
Property description prompt: Tell everyone why you love this property.

Errors that say what happened

SituationCopy
Name too longCollection name has a max of 32 characters
Duplicate nameA collection with this name already exists
Prohibited words in nameCollection names can't include prohibited language
Description too longDescription has a max of 250 characters
Prohibited words in descriptionDescriptions can't include prohibited language
Generic failureSomething went wrong. You can try again now or come back in a few minutes.
Empty collection, trying to shareAdd at least one stay to share this collection.
Hit the stay capYou can only save 15 stays to each collection.

Name the constraint instead of “Invalid input.” Don’t moralize prohibited language. Give a next step on failures. Match the error to the field.

High-stakes confirms

Changing a public collection to Only you breaks the Shop and every shared link. The confirm has to say that before they tap.

Are you sure you want to set the visibility to ‘Only you’? This collection won’t be visible on your Travel Shop and links to this collection will no longer work.
Delete Destinations I Adore and all 14 properties? Links to this collection will no longer work. Links you shared to individual properties will still work.

The second sentence is the design: people need to know what survives. “This can’t be undone” is not enough.

What this shows

Product UI for a two-sided travel experience. Systems: one vocabulary, many states. Judgment: when to be short, when to explain the consequence.
